ReachSocial stores client identifiers, hashed API keys, request metadata, hashed inputs, usage, payment receipts, and operational logs needed to provide, secure, and troubleshoot the service. It does not download or persist requested media.

Wallet sign-in stores the public wallet address, a short-lived session, and connected-agent records. Wallet signatures, recovery phrases, and x402 payment signatures are not retained. A paid request stores its service, normalized input, payment reference, and result-recovery record for up to 30 days. Uncertain settlement or provider-dispatch records stay available for reconciliation until they are resolved.
