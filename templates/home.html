WHY REACHSOCIAL

Operationally boring—in a good way.

Provider-neutral

Stable service IDs and normalized response shapes isolate clients from provider-specific payload changes.

Agent-discoverable

OpenAPI, llms.txt, agent.md, and an RFC 9727 API catalog expose the same reviewed service definitions.

Bounded by design

Idempotency, concurrency limits, upstream spend caps, durable jobs, and replay protection constrain costly mistakes.

Media-light

ReachSocial returns source URLs and metadata. It does not download or retain requested social-media bytes.
